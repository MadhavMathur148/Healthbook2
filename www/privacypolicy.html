Privacy Policy

Healthbook · last updated 31 October 2026

What Healthbook stores

Healthbook stores the habits, tasks, logs, rewards and monthly summaries you create, plus the answers you give during sign-up (name, birth year, sex if you choose to share it, height, weight, goals, usual wake and bed times, and the devices you use).

Where it is stored

  • Without an account: everything stays on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • With an account: an encrypted copy is kept on Healthbook's secure cloud storage so it can sync between your devices and be restored if you lose your phone. Access rules mean only your signed-in account can read or change it. No one else, including other Healthbook users, can see your data.

Health Connect data

If you connect Health Connect, Healthbook reads steps, sleep, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability and weight. It only reads; it never writes to Health Connect. This data is used only to fill in your logs, charts and monthly summaries. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone. You can disconnect at any time in Health Connect settings.

Monthly summaries

Summaries are calculated on your phone with fixed rules. No artificial intelligence and no third party is involved. A summary only leaves your phone when you choose to share it.

Your choices

  • Export a full backup at any time from the Me tab.
  • Delete your account from the Me tab. This permanently removes your profile, synced data and summaries from the cloud.
  • Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on your phone.

Not medical advice

Healthbook helps you track habits and spot patterns. It does not diagnose or treat any condition. Talk to a doctor about anything that concerns you.
